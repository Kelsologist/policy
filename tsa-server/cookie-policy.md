## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website or use an application. They help the Service recognize your device and remember certain information about your visit.

Similar technologies include:
- **Web beacons / pixel tags** — tiny images used to track page visits and email opens
- **Local storage / session storage** — browser-based storage for app state
- **Device fingerprinting** — combination of device attributes used for identification

- [Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/cookies-information-websites-store-on-your-computer)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-sfri11471/mac)
- [Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09)

### In-App Consent Manager
Where required by law (e.g., GDPR, ePrivacy Directive), we provide a cookie consent banner that allows you to accept or reject non-essential cookies.
