# Data Protection, Retention, and Deletion Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-08-29  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-29  
**Version:** 1.0.0

This policy describes how we protect data, how long we retain it, and how deletion requests are handled.

## Scope
Applies to personal and service-related data collected through our website and app.

## Data Protection Controls
- Access controls and least-privilege permissions
- Encryption in transit and at rest where applicable
- Logging, monitoring, and incident response processes

## Retention Principles
- Keep only what is necessary for service and legal obligations
- Review retention schedules periodically
- Remove or anonymize data when no longer required

## User Deletion Requests
Users may request data deletion through account settings or by contacting support.

## Deletion Workflow
1. Request is received and validated
2. Account and associated personal data are queued for deletion
3. Operational systems are cleared within defined SLA
4. Backups age out according to backup retention windows

## Target Timelines
- Initial response: within 7 days
- Completion target: within 30 days, unless legal exception applies

## Legal Exceptions
We may retain limited data where required by law, fraud prevention, security, or dispute resolution.

## Contact
For deletion or data rights requests, contact: `privacy@example.com`
