This Cookie Policy explains how cookies and similar technologies are used.

## What Are Cookies
Cookies are small text files placed on a device to support site operation, analytics, and preferences.

## Types We May Use
- **Essential cookies:** required for core functionality
- **Preference cookies:** remember settings
- **Analytics cookies:** help understand usage and improve performance

## Managing Cookies
You can control cookies through browser settings and consent tools where available.

## If You Do Not Use Non-Essential Cookies
If your app currently uses only essential cookies, document that here and update this policy before enabling analytics/marketing cookies.
