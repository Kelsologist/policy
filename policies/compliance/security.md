# Security Policy Summary

**Effective date:** 2026-08-29  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-29  
**Version:** 1.0.0

This document summarizes security practices supporting confidentiality, integrity, and availability.

## Administrative Controls
- Security ownership and accountability
- Access reviews and role-based access control
- Security awareness and incident procedures

## Technical Controls
- Encryption in transit and at rest where applicable
- Centralized logging and monitoring
- Vulnerability and patch management
- Backup and recovery procedures

## Incident Response
- Detect, triage, contain, and remediate incidents
- Notify affected users/regulators as required
- Perform post-incident review and corrective actions

## Review Cycle
This policy is reviewed on material system or legal changes and at least annually.
